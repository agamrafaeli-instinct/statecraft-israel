# Military and combat (war of 1948)

## Player story

On 15 May 1948, with the Provisional government declared, the player takes command of the Haganah as it becomes the Israel Defence Forces [S1]. Arab armies enter across every border, and the question the whole run has been building toward arrives at once: hold the country. The player moves battalion-size units across the map they already know, recruits fresh soldiers through the call-up and training they built in the struggle years, and spends the Arms their smuggling operations bought. Every front fight is decided by a strength table and one draw of fate, and the goal is never to destroy the enemy — it is to hold until the armistices are signed, one front at a time, until Syria signs last on 20 July 1949 [S2].

## Rules

- A unit is a formation of hundreds of fighters, never a single soldier (impact spec §2.2 [S8]; `Q-UNIT-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A finished recruit cohort becomes one unit at its city's settlement hex. Unit types on screen are Recruit (חייל חדש), Veteran soldier (חייל ותיק), Artillery (ארטילריה) and Armoured car (משוריין) — names approved in English and Hebrew (`Q-UI-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]). A recruit promotes to veteran after a number of turns of service (labelled design value); combat does not count toward it (`Q-UNIT-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit's Strength counts its population units and drives movement allowance and combat strength (`Q-UNIT-1`); where a side's historical strength is sourced, the record enters the content as one value per CONTENT_POLICY §1 (`Q-UNIT-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Historical mode seeds the player's starting units from `content/map/units_1948.yaml`, each keyed to a sourced anchor (`Q-START-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The Arab armies, the Arab Liberation Army and local Arab forces take part under their own names, with their 15 May 1948 positions seeded from the Phase five research file [S9] (`Q-START-4`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]; `D-G1`). Every row is fact-checked against its source before the `-shareable` tag (`Q-START-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). There are no air or naval units; where a phase needs them they are presentation-only (`Q-UNIT-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Open mode starts with no units (`Q-START-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Orders are free and spend no Attention (`D-A6`). The standing orders are Move (תנועה), Fortify (ביצור), Hold (החזקה) and Attack (התקפה) (`Q-UI-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]). Fortify takes one turn, costs nothing, and grants a defence bonus (value: open (owner)); the bonus is lost the moment the unit moves (`Q-HOLD-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]). Hold can be given only on the player's own hex and grants no interception (`Q-HOLD-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S7]).
- Movement costs integer movement points per unit type against an integer cost per biome; lakes and the sea are impassable (`Q-MOVE-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A built corridor road lowers its hex's movement cost, since roads mattered (`D-G2`; `Q-MOVE-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A stacking limit caps units of a side per hex; entering a hex an enemy unit holds is an attack (`Q-MOVE-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). There is no zone of control — a unit may enter any land hex it can pay for (`Q-MOVE-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit may not enter an enemy-held locality: entering would be a capture action, and the MVP records no rule for civilians under capture, so this is refused until Phase six (`Q-CIV-1`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]). There is no transport or embarkation (`Q-MOVE-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Combat follows the GDD: a deterministic table on the strength ratio, plus one RNG draw per engagement (`Q-COMBAT-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit attacks an adjacent enemy unit; attacks from several directions add their strengths; the defender's biome and fortify state modify the ratio (`Q-COMBAT-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Outcomes take losses from the table by population units; a losing defender retreats one hex; at strength zero a unit is removed; a defender with no retreat hex surrenders; the attacker does not advance into the empty hex in the MVP (`Q-COMBAT-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Combat acts on combatant units only — combat, bombing and conquest never affect a locality's residents (`Q-CIV-2`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]; CONTENT_POLICY §2).
- Recruits arrive only through the call-up at a military camp (`D-C4`). Artillery and armoured cars are acquired through training at a military camp, costing Arms and Money, where their forces are also refilled (`Q-ARTY-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Training costs Arms; an attack spends Arms per attacking unit, and a shortfall refuses the order with a reason (`D-G6`; `Q-COMBAT-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A unit costs Money upkeep each month (labelled design value), never Arms (`Q-UNIT-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]).
- Artillery adds its strength to an attack within its range and never attacks alone or holds ground; firing it spends Arms (`Q-ARTY-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The armoured car carries more movement points and its own combat factor (`Q-ARTY-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Artillery's on-screen name is Artillery (ארטילריה) until a sourced gun type arrives (`Q-ARTY-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]); which vehicles and guns are modelled stays open (`Q-ART-2`).
- Enemy forces act in the first resolver step, before the player's planned actions (impact spec §2.3 [S8]). In Historical mode they follow a script from two-source positions at 15 May and 11 June 1948 [S9], then act on simple goals; in Open mode goals only (`Q-ENEMY-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A goal is: hold a fortified position, or attack an adjacent enemy unit when the strength ratio meets a threshold; no RNG (`Q-ENEMY-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Enemy armies receive no reinforcements until a dated per-army figure is sourced (`Q-ENEMY-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Enemy units need no supply (`Q-ENEMY-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The four operation cards act only through orders to units already on the board; the sensitivity review resets which units receive which orders (`Q-ENEMY-5`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]).
- The war state is what war events set; in the GDD game loop the security order and call-up are refused with their reason (`Q-WAR-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; `D-C4`). The clock that forced war after fifty-two weeks from March 1948 is retired (`Q-WAR-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). During a truce attacks are refused with "a truce is in force"; movement and fortify are allowed. The refusal lasts from the first_truce acceptance until the turn covering 8 July 1948, and from the second_truce acceptance until the turn covering 15 October 1948 — the dates the fighting resumed in the MFA chronology [S10] (`Q-WAR-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The `set_rung` command keeps acting outside the GDD loop (`Q-WAR-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A war loss is enemy units holding a player's GDD city hex at the end of a turn (`Q-WAR-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]); Phase 7 turns it into the defeat ending.
- Prisoners are the only captives (impact spec §4.3 [S8]; `Q-POW-1`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S5]). Only combat surrender creates them (`Q-POW-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]). Each returned population unit scores points (value: open (owner)); the return happens on a sourced event card, one per front, and the prisoners' return is never the player's action (`Q-POW-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]). The player's captured soldiers are counted, are returned at the exchanges, and cost while held, with no score effect (`Q-POW-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]). Each prisoner costs Food per turn (value: open (owner)); a shortfall costs Social cohesion and never reduces prisoners (`Q-POW-5`, decided 15 October 2026 [S5]; CONTENT_POLICY §2).
- The war objective completes on the last armistice (`D-B2`): one hundred points, plus five points for every month earlier than January 1952, capped at sixty points (`D-B2`, `D-B6` [S1]). Each front's armistice card has its hard anchor at the agreement's date; it may open early as a soft event once the first turn on or after 16 November 1948 is reached on which that front has been quiet for a number of turns (labelled design value; the Security Council resolution of 16 November 1948 [S2]). A refused card returns when the front is quiet again (`Q-OBJ-1` and `Q-OBJ-2`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). The completion card's event is the Syria armistice of 20 July 1949 [S2]; if no suitably licensed archival photograph is found, the card shows no photograph; the card's losses line reports the Israeli total only (`Q-OBJ-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]; `Q-CIV-4` as re-ruled, 15 October 2026 [S6]). On a front's armistice, that front's units leave the board, the player's units are free to move, and the armistice lines stay display-only (`Q-OBJ-4`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). Losses are shown as people per engagement, in the Why line (`Q-CIV-4`, the owner's first ruling, 15 October 2026 [S4]). Kills are never a score or a resource (`Q-CIV-3`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S4]; CONTENT_POLICY §2).
- The attack preview shows the strength ratio and the table's outcome bands for it, from a sim helper (`Q-UI-1`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A token shows three figures at two thirds or more of full strength, two at a third or more, one below that (`Q-UI-3`, decided 15 October 2026 [S3]). A side with no sourced flag draws a neutral pennant until a sourced emblem is found (`Q-ART-1`, the owner's pick, 15 October 2026 [S5]).

- Enemy reinforcements — `Q-ENEMY-3`.

## Open questions
